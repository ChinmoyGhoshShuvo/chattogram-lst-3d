**Title:** Chattogram District April Heat Surface, 2015–2025 (3D LST)

**Summary:** I built yearly 30 m April land surface temperature composites for Chattogram District from 91 Landsat 8/9 scenes and turned them into an animated 3D heat surface in QGIS. The city core, the port/EPZ belt and the airport are the hottest ground every year. The district median swings between 33.0 °C and 37.3 °C with pre-monsoon weather, with no warming trend.
